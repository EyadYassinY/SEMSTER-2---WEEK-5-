My Favourite Dish: Shawarma

  1. Ingredients: Pita/arab bread, gralic sauce, pickled cucumbers (preferibly Sweet) shreddered chicken/beef/lamb and Fresh Fries
  2. Mix the marinade: Combine the olive oil, lemon juice, yogurt, minced garlic, and all the dry spices in a large bowl or ziplock bag.
  3. Marinate the chicken: Add the chicken thighs and rub the mixture thoroughly into the meat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours, or up to 24 hours for best results
  4. Heat the pan: Heat a large cast-iron skillet or non-stick pan over medium-high heat with a splash of neutral oil
  5. Cook the chicken: Add the chicken in a single layer without overcrowding. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes on the first side until deeply golden and charred, then flip and cook for another 3 to 4 minutes until cooked through.
  6. Rest and slice: Let the chicken rest on a cutting board for 5 minutes, then slice it thinly.
  7. Assemble: Serve warm wrapped in flatbread with pickles, tomatoes, and your favorite sauce
